Every open work order's operations, laid onto shop hours, the crew and the machines in queue order: rush and high priority first, then the earliest promised or due date, unless a work order has been moved in the queue below. Operations pinned to a day come first that day, and an operation can run on another machine in its group when that finishes it sooner. It's worked out afresh each time, from planned time less time logged. Work orders waiting on something aren't planned until they resume.
