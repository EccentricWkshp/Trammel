Online payments

Customers pay invoices online, by card or bank through Stripe. Trammel makes a payment link for each open invoice's balance, shown in the customer portal and in invoice emails, and asks Stripe every minute what was paid, recording each payment against its invoice. Nothing has to reach this server from the internet.

Setting it up

  1. In Stripe's dashboard (Developers, API keys), make a restricted key with:
      {% for name, level in permissions %}
    • {{ name }}: {{ level }}
      • {% endfor %}
    Products and Prices let each link carry its invoice's amount. Start with a test key (in test mode) and pay an invoice with card 4242 4242 4242 4242 to see it work.
  2. Paste the key below, switch payments on, and save. Links are made within a minute.

Stripe's fees come out of what you receive; customers pay the invoice amount. A refund is made in Stripe's dashboard, then recorded here as a payment of a negative amount. Customers get Trammel's usual payment receipt by email.
