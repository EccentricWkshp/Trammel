Online payments

Customers pay invoices online, through Stripe (card or bank) and PayPal. Trammel makes each open invoice a page to pay its balance at each one that's on, shown in the customer portal and in invoice emails, and asks every minute what was paid, recording each payment against its invoice. Nothing has to reach this server from the internet.

  1. On developer.paypal.com, Apps & Credentials, create an app under Sandbox (and later under Live). Then open it, tick Invoicing under its features, and Save. Copy its client ID and secret.
  2. Paste them below with Sandbox ticked, switch PayPal on, save, and test the credentials. If PayPal refuses, the test says whether it's the app (Invoicing not ticked) or its PayPal account.
  3. Then pay an invoice from the portal with a sandbox personal account to see it work, and swap in the live app's credentials with Sandbox unticked.

Each invoice gets one PayPal invoice, numbered exactly as in Trammel and sent to nobody (only its pay page is used); when the balance changes it's updated, and once it's no longer owed it's cancelled. PayPal can't send customers back to the portal after paying.
