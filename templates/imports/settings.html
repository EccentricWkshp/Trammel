Shopify

Setting up the app in Shopify
  1. Make an app for your store: a custom app in the store's admin (Settings, Apps and sales channels, Develop apps), or an app in the Dev Dashboard.
  2. Give it these Admin API scopes: read_orders, read_customers, read_fulfillments and read_products. Shopify gives the last 60 days of orders; for older ones, ask for read_all_orders too.
  3. In the Dev Dashboard, release that version and install the app on the store, so the scopes are approved.
  4. Enter the Dev Dashboard app's client ID and secret here, or the custom app's access token.

Use the Dev Dashboard app with its client ID and secret if you can: below Shopify's Advanced plan, a custom app made in the store's admin gets no buyer names, emails or addresses, while a Dev Dashboard app does. Orders without their buyer wait on the Online orders page for you to give them a customer.

Etsy

Etsy collects and pays the sales tax on its sales as the marketplace facilitator. Etsy sales show that tax, and the tax report lists it as paid by Etsy, not as tax you owe.

Setting up the app on Etsy
  1. On the Etsy developer site (Your apps), create an app for your shop. It gives a keystring and a shared secret.
  2. Add this callback address to the app: {{ etsy_callback }}
  3. Enter the keystring and shared secret below, tick Connect Etsy, and save.
  4. Press Connect Etsy and approve Trammel on Etsy. Etsy sends you back here.

If Etsy can't send you back (this browser can't reach Trammel at that address), copy the address Etsy sent you to and paste it below. Etsy keeps the connection for 90 days after it was last used; after that, connect again.

eBay

eBay collects and pays the sales tax on its sales as the marketplace facilitator; eBay sales record it that way. eBay withholds the buyer's details on some orders, which then wait on the Online orders page for a customer.

Setting up the app on eBay
  1. On the eBay developer site, under Application Keys, use the production keyset: its App ID (Client ID) and Cert ID (Client Secret). Tick the sandbox below only for eBay's test keys.
  2. Under User Tokens, choose "Get a Token from eBay via Your Application" and add a redirect (RuName). Set its auth accepted URL to {{ ebay_callback }}, and enter the RuName below.
  3. eBay won't switch a keyset on for real orders until you deal with its account deletion notices. Trammel can't stand at a public address for them; see "Account deletion notices" below.
  4. Save, then press Connect eBay and approve Trammel on eBay. eBay sends you back here.

If eBay can't send you back, copy the address it sent you to and paste it below. A connection lasts about 18 months; after that, connect again.

Account deletion notices

eBay tells every app when an eBay account closes, and the buyer's data has to go. eBay needs a public HTTPS address that answers its challenge, which Trammel doesn't host: a small relay on the internet can take eBay's notices and forward each one to Trammel's API (POST /api/v1/imports/ebay/deletion-notices, with an API key holding only "Forward eBay account deletion notices"). You can also enter a notice by hand.

When a notice arrives, the buyer's details in the stored eBay orders are wiped at once. Their customer record waits for you to anonymize it or dismiss the request; sales and tax figures always stay.
